Welcome to SAR

Saudi Arabia Railways (الخطوط الحديدية السعودية) is the national railway operator of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, connecting major cities with modern, fast, and comfortable trains.

Our mission is to provide safe, efficient, and eco-friendly transportation across the Kingdom, linking Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam, Jeddah and more.

Use the menu above to explore destinations, view our gallery, create an account, or book your next trip with us.
